# Submission freeze policy

## Before final Devpost submission

Artifacts, README, video package, repository content and hosted application may be corrected during human review. Product behavior remains frozen: only a proven bug may authorize a functional change.

## After final Devpost submission

Until winners are announced, do not modify the submitted or linked:

- video or visibility;
- repository branch, tag or content;
- hosted application behavior;
- screenshots or architecture;
- Devpost text or links.

If continued development is desired, fork to an unlinked repository and do not change the submitted release candidate. The only exception is a specific written instruction from the organizers permitting a narrow correction.

## Activation

This lock is **documented but not active**. It activates only when the human performs the final Devpost submission. Phase 5 does not submit.
