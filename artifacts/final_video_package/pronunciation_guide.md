# TTS pronunciation guide

Use this guide during voice selection and segment review. The spelling hints are for an English-language voice, not replacements for the written brand names.

| Written term | Desired spoken form | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ShiftChain | “Shift Chain” | Two clear words; no pause long enough to split the brand. |
| Gemini | “JEM-in-eye” | Stress the first syllable. |
| Firestore | “FIRE-store” | Stress “fire.” |
| Cloud Tasks | “Cloud Tasks” | Natural English; keep the final consonants clear. |
| Google ADK | “Google A-D-K” | Say A, D, and K as separate letters. |
| HTTP 503 | “H-T-T-P five-oh-three” | Say every letter and use “oh” for zero. |
| NO_OP_VERIFIED | “no-op verified” | Never speak the underscores or the raw constant. |
| Maya | “MY-uh” | Two syllables. |
| Liam | “LEE-um” | Two syllables. |
| Sofia | “soh-FEE-uh” | Three syllables; stress the middle syllable. |
| Noah | “NOH-uh” | Two syllables. |
| Emma | “EM-uh” | Two syllables. |
| Cloud Run | “Cloud Run” | Natural English; do not spell either word. |
| Secret Manager | “Secret Manager” | Natural English; light stress on “Secret.” |

## Review checklist

- Confirm the selected voice does not merge “Maya-to-Liam” into an unclear name.
- Confirm “A-D-K” is spoken letter by letter.
- Confirm “HTTP five-oh-three” is intelligible at normal playback speed.
- Confirm the reliability segment says “no-op verified,” never the raw constant.
- Confirm a noticeable pause follows “I will not click anything.”
