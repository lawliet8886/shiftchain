# Final human checklist

## Review

- [ ] Approve the English narration and pronunciation.
- [ ] Confirm eligibility and entrant identity/team details.
- [ ] Confirm Taskmaster as the single category.
- [ ] Decide whether Individual/Hobbyist and Best Architectural Design selections are accurate and available.

## Record and upload

- [ ] Run `/health` and queue preflight.
- [ ] Record one continuous real demo at 1×, target 3:35–3:42, never above 4:00.
- [ ] Verify the recording shows `.run.app`, waiting, zero-click resume, intentional 503, retry and `NO_OP_VERIFIED`.
- [ ] Review the complete video for secrets, notifications, audio and legibility.
- [ ] Upload to YouTube or Vimeo as **Public**.
- [ ] Paste the final public URL into `project_links.json` and the Devpost draft/form.

## Devpost

- [ ] Join/register for the hackathon with the intended Devpost account.
- [ ] Copy the reviewed English project fields.
- [ ] Add the public GitHub, hosted app, architecture and public video URLs.
- [ ] Add only confirmed optional contribution URLs/prize considerations.
- [ ] Preview the full submission and test every link.
- [ ] Final-submit only after explicit human approval.
- [ ] Activate the submission freeze and leave all linked assets unchanged through judging.

## Optional bonus

- [ ] Review and optionally publish the article draft publicly with the required hackathon-entry disclosure.
- [ ] Review and optionally publish the LinkedIn draft with `#AllThingsAgenticHackathon`.
- [ ] Add those URLs only if they are genuinely public and accurate.
